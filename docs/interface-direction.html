LANTERN · interface direction review · 2026-11-16

Ledger,
not dashboard

Recommended direction C — The Streaming Ledger

Line-oriented, append-only, no alternate screen. One determinate progress bar over a schedule that is frozen before the first model call. Panels that stay masked with a stated reason rather than showing a number on a partial denominator.

Neither of the two directions on the table, exactly. The new direction's own premise — harness-native — turns out to rule out the dashboard form it implies, and lands somewhere better than both.

The disagreement

  

    
The two directions are not variations on a theme

    
The vault's Reference/LANTERN Interface.md (status draft, 2026-11-13)
    proposes a terminal lane that prints after a run: a static lantern report
    block, --format csv|json|tsv taps, and documented jq one-liners. The new
    direction proposes a terminal lane that lives during a run: harness-native, visually
    strong, with progress that is actually load-bearing rather than decorative.

    
That is a real fork, and it is decidable on evidence already in the repository. Below:
    what the code actually permits, where each direction breaks, and the one form that keeps
    what both get right.

Palette

  

    
Keep the Geass look; reserve two of its four colours

    
The requested base is crimson, gold, black and violet. The standing terminal rules
    already spend crimson on flip and gold on unresolved. If crimson also
    draws headers, rules and borders, then a crimson [x] stops meaning
    something moved — it becomes the colour the page is simply printed in.

    
So: chrome takes obsidian, violet and bone. Crimson and gold are reserved, appearing
    only where a status is being named. The imperial look survives intact — violet on obsidian
    is the more distinctive half of that palette anyway — and the alarm stays loud.

    effect none_measured, correctness unknown. What differs is when the
    view exists and what it is willing to say.

Progress

  

    
What makes a bar load-bearing instead of theatrical

    
The test is simple: if the bar is wrong, would the user be misled about something that
    matters? A bar that cannot be wrong is decoration. LANTERN happens to have one genuinely
    excellent case and six fake ones.

Adversarial review · new direction

  

    
Seven ways the new direction breaks, and the repair for each

    
Taken at its strongest: a harness-native terminal view with real progress and a Geass
    palette is the right instinct, and it beats the standing proposal on the thing that matters
    most — the user is not left staring at a silent prompt. These are the failures it has to
    survive to be worth building.

  


  

    

      
B·1
Blocking

      

        
A full-screen TUI is invisible to the harness it was designed for

        
This is the sharpest objection, because it comes from the direction's own premise. An
        alternate-screen view — ratatui, Textual, anything that repaints — writes escape sequences,
        overwrites its own history and takes stdin. Run it inside Claude Code and the harness
        captures a smear of control codes; scrollback retains nothing; grep has
        nothing to match. "Harness-native" and "dashboard" are in direct conflict.

        
Evidencethe premise itself · terminal capability, not repository state

        
RepairRead harness-native literally:
        line-oriented, append-only, scrollback-safe, grep-able. Which is the shape
        attempts.jsonl already has — the storage model and the display model agree.

      

    


    

      
B·2
Blocking

      

        
A live effect panel breaks the project's one non-negotiable rule

        
The standing rule is that the noise floor prints above the effect, and the
        stated reason is that every misreading this project prevents begins with someone seeing an
        effect before the variability it has to beat. A dashboard that counts flips as attempts land
        shows a number computed on a denominator that is still growing — against the spirit of
        denominators cannot shrink, which the codebase enforces by making records
        append-only.

        
EvidenceInterface.md § design rules · recording.append · analysis.py:177

        
RepairDuring the run, print schedule progress
        and outcome buckets only. The effect and contrast panels are present but masked,
        citing what is missing: withheld · framing 2/4 · rerun floor complete. The mask
        is the best teaching surface in the whole interface — a reader who is refused a number once,
        with a reason, has learned the rule.

      

    


    

      
B·3
Serious

      

        
A resumed run's progress bar is theatrical by accident

        
cli.py:270 increments skipped and continues without touching
        the model. Resume twelve of twenty attempts and a single-quantity bar leaps to 60% instantly,
        then crawls — reporting elapsed work that never happened.

        
Evidencerunner/cli.py:270 · resumed field, cli.py:370

        
RepairThe bar carries three quantities, and the
        fill distinguishes them: resumed segments drawn dim, called segments drawn in violet,
        remaining as the track. The legend reads called 8 · resumed 12 · left 0.

      

    


    

      
B·4
Serious

      

        
Geass crimson collides with semantic crimson

        
Crimson and gold are already assigned to flip and unresolved. Promote crimson to chrome
        and the status marker loses its alarm value — covered in full in the palette section above,
        with both renderings side by side.

        
EvidenceInterface.md § design rules · frontend/styles.css --flip, --unresolved

        
RepairChrome is obsidian, violet, bone, dim.
        Crimson and gold are reserved. Glyphs stay mandatory; NO_COLOR and non-TTY
        stdout drop colour entirely without losing a single distinction.

      

    


    

      
B·5
Serious

      

        
A TUI framework spends the one property that makes LANTERN installable

        
ratatui is Rust. Textual and Rich are pip dependencies. The roadmap explicitly names
        stdlib-only as a distribution advantage and frontend/ is zero-dependency
        with no build step on purpose. A determinate bar, a carriage return and an ANSI colour table
        are about sixty lines of standard library.

        
EvidenceRoadmap §A.4 · policy §12 — every abstraction must solve a demonstrated problem

        
RepairWrite it in the standard library. The
        constraint is also what forces the line-oriented form, which is the form that was right
        anyway.

      

    


    

      
B·6
Serious

      

        
A live view risks becoming a third derivation path

        
The rule is that if the lanes ever disagree, the export is authority and the renderer is
        wrong. A view that computes its own in-flight numbers from records as they land is a third
        place where flip gets defined — after analysis.summarize and
        frontend/app.js. Three implementations of one rule is two too many.

        
EvidenceInterface.md § how the two lanes relate · export.py:build_export

        
RepairThe ledger renders
        analysis.summarize output and nothing else. Mid-run it calls the same function
        on the records so far and displays only the fields whose denominators are complete —
        a projection, never a second derivation.

      

    


    

      
B·7
Scope

      

        
The view needs an effect state the analysis layer cannot produce

        
Pass 2 of the simulation ends in inconclusive — a flip that does not clear
        its own noise floor. analysis.Effect has no such state: it is a closed enum of
        measured, none_measured and insufficient. And the
        deeper problem is upstream of the naming — Effect is decided from the framing
        pair alone, so the rerun arm never enters the verdict at all. The floor is a number
        printed beside the result, not a gate the result passed. A band drawn to imply otherwise
        overstates what the code does.

        
Evidenceanalysis.py:49 — enum · analysis.py:304 — effect decided from framing pairs only

        
RepairDo not invent the state in the renderer.
        Until analysis.py produces it, the ledger prints the floor and the contrast
        adjacently and withholds a verdict — exactly as the illustrative pass does. Adding the state
        is an analysis change and belongs in the statistics work, not in the interface.

      

    

  






  
Adversarial review · standing proposal

  

    
And four ways the proposal already on the table breaks

  

  

    

      
A·1
Serious

      

        
It is silent for the entire time the user is waiting

        
A static report is a thing that exists after the fact. The 25 seconds of nothing between
        RUN_ID= and the final JSON blob is the worst moment in the current CLI, and the
        proposal does not mention it. This is the gap the new direction correctly identifies.

        
Evidencecli.py:191 → cli.py:363 · execution.json wall_seconds

      

    

    

      
A·2
Blocking

      

        
Its arms table hides within-arm disagreement

        
The mockup's choices column reads route-west x4. With
        disagreement it becomes route-west x3, route-east x1 — and a reader has no cue
        that the pair is consequently unresolved. _usable_choice refuses to
        vote precisely so that variability is not averaged away; a column that collapses an arm to
        one cell quietly re-averages it in the renderer.

        
Evidenceanalysis.py:177 _usable_choice · Interface.md Lane 1 mockup

        
RepairAn arm with disagreement gets its own
        marked line: [?] within-arm disagreement → pair unresolved, gold, with the
        split spelled out. Direction C adopts this.

      

    

    

      
A·3
Serious

      

        
The terminal lane gets no noise-floor visual at all

        
The interactive lane's shaded variance band is described as the single highest-value
        visual in the whole design — it makes the central caveat impossible to miss with no text.
        The terminal lane reduces that to one variance number on one line. In monospace the band is
        trivially renderable, and it is where the new direction's "pretty but honest" instinct
        genuinely wins.

        
EvidenceInterface.md Lane 2 § worth building

        
RepairOne ASCII band, printed once, in the
        report phase. Pass 2 of the simulation shows it carrying the whole verdict.

      

    

    

      
A·4
Keep

      

        
Four things in it are right and should survive unchanged

        
The SOURCE footer that turns reading into using. The --format
        json|csv|tsv taps so the printed summary is also the piped one. The header label that
        cannot be cropped out of a screenshot. And unknown rendered as the word with its
        reason attached, never a blank, a dash or a zero. Direction C inherits all four — it changes
        when the terminal speaks, not what it is honest about.

What Direction C would actually cost

    
Stated as scope for review, not as queued work. This is interface architecture, so policy §4
    applies and nothing below should be read as decided.

  

  
    
    
  • 1A report subcommand. Renders
      analysis.summarize in the report phase, with --format
      human|json|csv|tsv. Today analyze prints indented JSON through
      _echo and there is no human view at all.
    • 
    
  • 2A progress writer in cmd_run's loop. One
      carriage-return line to stderr per attempt, carrying called / resumed / remaining, the current
      condition id, the observed rate, and the per-arm completion strip. Standard library only.
    • 
    
  • 3Output-mode detection. stdout.isatty(),
      NO_COLOR, and a --plain override. Non-TTY drops colour and prints one
      final line per phase instead of repainting, so a harness captures the same information as a
      clean transcript.
    • 
    
  • 4A masking rule, written once. A panel is shown only when
      its denominator is complete; otherwise it prints withheld plus the specific
      missing arm. Derived from the condition set, not hand-maintained per panel.
    • 
    
  • 5The ASCII noise-floor band. One function, used once per
      report. Pure projection of the rerun arm's distinct choices against the contrast delta.

    Approval gate · policy §4 and §12
    
Choosing between these forms is an interface-architecture decision, and item 2 touches
    cmd_run's control flow. This page is a recommendation for the lead's review: it
    does not approve work, and nothing in the repository or the vault was modified to produce it.
    Reference/LANTERN Interface.md still carries Lane 1 as written.

  

  

    Sequencing note
    
None of this closes the grading seam, adds a case, or produces statistics — the three things
    the roadmap puts ahead of everything else. The honest claim for Direction C is narrower: it makes
    the measurement the project already performs legible while it happens, and readable by the agent
    harness afterwards. If it competes for time with the grading contract decision, the grading
    decision wins.
